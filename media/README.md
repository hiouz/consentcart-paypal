## Upload order and captions

   **ConsentCart — informed checkout for agentic commerce.** The buyer defines the purchase and the limits before an AI-assisted checkout begins.

2. `02-review-decision.png`
   **A decision the buyer can inspect.** ConsentCart explains why this $79 purchase needs approval instead of returning an unexplained risk score.

3. `03-comprehension-gate.png`
   **Understanding is required before checkout.** The buyer confirms the amount, recurring commitment, and PayPal payment boundary.

4. `04-verified-audit-trail.png`

5. `05-blocked-renewal.png`
   **Hard rules stay outside the model.** A recurring purchase is blocked before PayPal is contacted because it violates the buyer's policy.

Use `01-consentcart-cover.png` as the first gallery image and social preview.
